## Category 2: Readings that produce different amounts

**A1. How a refund is divided on a split deal (FR-012, FR-017, and the partial-refund clarification).**
- **Reading (a), per-refund.** FR-017 says each refund is "divided among the reps by FR-012's allocation". So a rep's remaining credit is their original share minus their allocated share of each refund.
- **Reading (b), reduced deal.** The partial-refund clarification says to "recompute the booking quarter with the deal amount reduced by the refunded amount". So the reduced deal is re-split with the largest-remainder rule.
- **Input:** quota $100,000 each. Deal $10,000.20, split 50/50 (A listed first). Original shares are 5,000.10 each, and each earns 250.005, which rounds to 250.01. The deal is refunded $100.01 in the quarter.
  - Under (a): the refund splits 50.01 to A and 50.00 to B. A's credit becomes 4,950.09, earning 247.5045 → 247.50, so **A's clawback is $2.51**. B's credit becomes 4,950.10, earning 247.505 → 247.51, so **B's clawback is $2.50**.
  - Under (b): the remaining 9,900.19 splits as 4,950.095 each, and the leftover cent goes to A. A gets 4,950.10, so **A's clawback is $2.50**. B gets 4,950.09, so **B's clawback is $2.51**.
- **Reading (a) can also go wrong.** Take a $0.03 deal split 50/50 (A 0.02, B 0.01) with three $0.01 refunds. Each refund's cent goes to A on the tie. A's credit ends at **−$0.01**, which FR-012 never contemplates.
- **Verdict:** a genuine ambiguity. Reading (b) avoids the negative credit.

**A2. Does FR-011's start-date check apply to deals that are not counted? (FR-011, FR-008, Edge Cases)**
- **Input:** quarter Q1 2026. The rep starts 2026-01-10 with $50k counted in the quarter. A second deal booked 2025-12-15 is credited to the same rep, so FR-008 excludes it.
- **Reading 1:** any deal listing the rep counts as "credited", as the edge-case wording suggests. The scenario is rejected, and **no payout is shown**.
- **Reading 2:** an excluded deal is not credited. The deal is just listed as excluded, and **the rep is paid $2,500 of commission**.

**A3. Earlier-quarter data that names a rep outside the scenario (FR-016, Edge Cases "rep not in scenario").**
- **Input:** a Q2 scenario includes Q1 data for rep A, because A's Q1 deal D1 was refunded in Q2. Another Q1 deal of A's, D2, is split with rep Y, who is not in the Q2 scenario.
- **Reading 1:** that is a deal credited to a rep who is not in the scenario, so the scenario is rejected with no payout.
- **Reading 2:** Y's share is simply fixed Q1 input, and **A's clawback is computed normally**.
- **Verdict:** narrower than A1 and A2, but the text does not settle it.
