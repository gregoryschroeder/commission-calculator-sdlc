- **Reading 2, merge A to 50% first:** A and B tie, and the tie goes to A as first listed, so A gets $5.01 and B $5.00.
- **Reading 3:** reject the scenario.

**B. A prorated quota that rounds to $0.00 (FR-005, FR-009, FR-010, FR-018).** Example: quota $0.01, a 91-day quarter, and a rep starting on the last day. $0.01 × 1/91 rounds to $0.00.
